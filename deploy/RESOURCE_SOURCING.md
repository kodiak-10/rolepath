# Resource sourcing and review

RolePath prioritizes original, inspectable materials on domestic platforms when they explicitly cover the user's job requirements. Foreign references remain supplementary. A Chinese page or a successful fetch is not proof of access from every mainland network; regional playback, login and paid access are disclosed rather than guaranteed.

## Admission criteria

- Keep a stable original URL, publisher, title, dates where available, explicit skill coverage, selected chapters, and review evidence.
- Prefer official documentation, original educators, university lectures with a traceable source, and practitioner articles with explicit scope. Popularity and creator identity are not quality scores.
- Record what was checked. Reading a catalogue or public API verifies metadata, not teaching quality or successful video playback. No unperformed full-course review is implied.
- Check freshness against the task. Old foundational material may remain useful, but outdated tool interfaces and APIs need a visible warning.
- Bilibili and Xiaohongshu are eligible sources, not automatic endorsements. A Xiaohongshu post needs a stable original link, identifiable author, relevant content and a clear access condition before admission. No Xiaohongshu post was added in this update because none was sufficiently verified.
- Do not fabricate chapter links, durations, corporate attribution, ratings, or interview questions. Unsupported requirements remain gaps.

## Question provenance

| Record | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `platform_problem` | A platform practice question, not corporate recruitment provenance |
| `company_bank` | A company-labelled practice bank maintained by the platform |
| `company_archive` | A historical corporate exam attributed by the hosting platform; employer confirmation is not implied |
| `interview_recall` | A user's recollection; not an employer-issued paper or an official answer |

Link to the original problem or archive and provide a short description and learning deliverable. Do not copy complete protected papers or bypass login, payment, or anti-bot restrictions. Record missing publication dates explicitly rather than inventing them.
